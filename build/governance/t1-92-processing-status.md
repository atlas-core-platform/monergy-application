## Scope and journey

Master sections4.2/33/34/35/36/41, MON-ADM-002/004, MON-JOB-001/003 and P1-28 require administrator queues, the six job states, failure/attempt visibility and authorized recovery. Organization Settings > Processing Status now delivers the complete operational journey: tenant-scoped queue and state counts, literal reference search, customer/state filters,50-item cursor pages, current customer name/secondary labels, job details, paged execution attempts and immutable administrator recovery history. It refreshes every15 seconds while visible, pauses during review or an uncertain request, and supports manual refresh. Customers links to the selected customer's processing view. Counts are operational counts, not financial totals.

All required states retain owner meaning: QUEUED, PROCESSING, COMPLETED, FAILED, RETRYING and CANCELLED. Retry scheduled or pending automatic retry is shown as Retrying. Running cancellation is a request, remains Processing, and is never reported as completed or cancelled without an owner-confirmed outcome. A failed/interrupted running cancellation retains unknown outcome and requires owner reconciliation; this console has no reconciliation override. Queued or pending retry work can be cancelled immediately. Completed, cancelled, nonretryable or uncertain-outcome jobs cannot be manually retried.

## Authority and recovery

The Job Management owner exposes GET queue/detail/attempts/history/exact receipt and POST changes under /local/v1/administration/processing, through the existing same-origin jobs-api Gateway allowlist. Current AM TenantAdmin authority is checked before owner access, immediately before writes and after reads/writes; tenant, actor, authority and policy context must match. Source jobs require the exact persisted server workload tenant and matching access customer; known foreign bindings are also excluded. Legacy engineering jobs without tenant provenance are not assigned a tenant by inference. All existing current product jobs are document-processing; generic recorded jobs remain Background task metadata and cannot gain a new retry producer.

Retry now requires a retryable failed document-processing job without lease/reconciliation, explicit reason and review, exact source revision, and current original-customer authorization and Consent preflight. It preserves the full original customer/security/payload context; the existing real owner-backed worker checks current authorization/Consent again before execution. Administrator authority never substitutes for customer permission. Original customer denial returns a409 review conflict rather than signing out a valid administrator. Dependency failures remain unavailable. Retry is disabled in adapter modes without the real owner-backed product execution policy. A saved exact receipt can still be recovered after original permission expires or retry capability is disabled, without re-enqueueing work.

An operation ID, source revision, action, customer snapshot and reason stay exact through uncertain close/reopen and same-tenant/actor reauthentication. Other writes are blocked until exact fresh saved receipt confirmation.400 permits correction;409 requires refresh and a new review. Lost post-commit authority fails closed and can recover the existing request after restoration. Changed actor/tenant/session hides old data and discards late responses. Returned metadata, actor/operation/reason, before/after transition and fresh receipt must all match; forged results cannot be confirmed.

Strict16KiB JSON rejects unknown/missing/null/duplicate fields; queries and cursors are bounded and validated. Responses are no-store/same-origin. Queue/attempt metadata omits source credentials, leases, payloads, documents and financial records. Current customer labels are fetched in one bounded tenant-scoped batch of at most50 IDs from Customer & Identity; missing/foreign customers remain explicitly unavailable. No N+1 customer HTTP requests or protected identity access are introduced.

## Persistence

In-memory and PostgreSQL adapters share their existing authoritative job instance. Additive Job Management migration0003 adds immutable administrator_operations with tenant/customer-scoped before/after metadata, actor, reason, operation and time; runtime grants SELECT/INSERT and denies UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE. Operation locks and the existing job row lock serialize recovery against workers; exact replay/conflict and repeatable-read queue/count pages retain owner consistency. Source revision hashes fence all owner state/context changes. Recovery timestamps are canonical PostgreSQL microsecond precision and the stored raw job is verified before committing its receipt. Pagination orders immutable requested time/reference, and remains valid when a cursor job changes status.
